About PHANTOM
PHANTOM is a free, open-source metasearch interface. It sends searches to publicly available SearXNG instances and displays the results they return. Search providers are independent third parties; availability, ranking and privacy practices depend on those providers. PHANTOM does not promise anonymous browsing or unlimited uptime.
